# Recipe 00: Getting Started – The Anti-Color-Poisoning Kitchen Reset
**Inspired by Julia Child's joyful, real-ingredient revolution — but we're going harder against synthetic colors and ultra-processed fakery.**

## Description
This isn't a dish yet — it's the mise en place for the whole book/project. Color poisoning (artificial dyes, ultra-processed visual/taste manipulation, neon snacks that trick the brain) is the enemy. Real food is the medicine. We start simple: recognize, remove, replace.

## Ingredients (metaphorical + literal first steps)
- 1 large awareness wake-up call (artificial colors are dopamine drugs)
- 2–3 days of reading labels like a detective
- A kitchen trash bag for the ultra-processed casualties
- Fresh whole foods: greens, roots, berries, herbs (the real colors)
- Optional: one vintage Julia Child cookbook for inspiration and courage

## Instructions
1. **Declare war on color poisoning**  
   Derek: "The book is about color poisoning and how to combat it with food just like Julia Child attempted back in the day. We are going to do better."  
   → This is the mission statement. Pin it above your stove.

2. **Audit your current kitchen**  
   Walk through your pantry/fridge. Anything neon red, electric blue, day-glo orange? Anything with Red 40, Yellow 5, Blue 1, Titanium Dioxide in the ingredients list? Into the bin it goes (or donate if sealed and you're feeling generous).

3. **Shop the perimeter like it's 1961**  
   Buy only foods that look like themselves:  
   - Deep green spinach/kale (chlorophyll power)  
   - Blood-red beets (natural betalains)  
   - Bright-but-real orange carrots (beta-carotene)  
   - Purple cabbage, turmeric yellow, cocoa brown  
   No Skittles, no rainbow cereals, no fluorescent energy drinks.

4. **First simple ritual meal**  
   Make a giant rainbow salad — but only using foods that grew that color naturally. Dress with olive oil + lemon + sea salt. Eat slowly while reading the back of an old ultra-processed package you kept for comparison.

5. **Document & iterate**  
   Take before/after photos of your fridge/pantry. Note how your energy, cravings, and even mood change after 3–7 days without synthetic colors. Report back here — this becomes Recipe 01.
